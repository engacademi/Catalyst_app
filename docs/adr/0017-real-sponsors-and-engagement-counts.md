# 0017 — Real OVA sponsors and anonymous engagement counts

2026-10-18

This builds on [ADR 0012](0012-event-sponsors-and-showcase.md). Its sponsor surfaces stay as designed: the Discover ribbon, the venue page's event partner carousel, and the sponsor messages that pop up on every second eligible page entry. What changes is the sponsors they show and the counting behind them.

Those surfaces now show OVA's 2026 Ontario Championships sponsors as OVA lists them at https://www.ontariovolleyball.org/ocs-sponsorship: Mikasa Sports Canada, Canuckstuff, Hudl, Hudson Travel Group, Six Pack Recruiting, Timu, Government of Ontario, Destination Toronto and City of Toronto. The logos are the files that page serves. They are trimmed, resized and kept in `_front/public/sponsors/` so the app doesn't depend on OVA's CDN. Links are the ones on the OVA page. OVA's page gives no links for Destination Toronto and City of Toronto, so those cards use each organization's own home page. The card descriptions are short and neutral and make no claims about sponsorship tiers.

`seed-testing` runs on every Railway deploy, and it now also syncs these cards into demo events (including the staff showcase) and events titled "Ontario Championships". The sync removes the earlier fictional placeholder sponsors (Example Sports Co., Rally Athletics, Courtside Hydration, Northstar Sport), adds any real sponsor that is missing, and fills in a missing logo or link. Staff edits to copy, order and visibility stay as they are. The showcase's "Red Bull · Demo" card is left in place, since ADR 0012 keeps it as an explicitly fictional demo placement.

R-23 engagement counts are aggregate counters per partner card and placement (`ribbon`, `guide` for the venue page carousel, or `message` for sponsor messages) in `partner_engagement` (migration 0006). The browser posts the card id, placement and action to `POST /v1/sponsor-engagement` and nothing else: no user id, device id, IP address or timestamp per visitor. A ribbon or carousel view is counted once per browser session per placement, when the sponsor is at least half on screen; a sponsor message counts a view when it appears. A click is counted each time, sent with `sendBeacon` so the outbound link opens without waiting. Cards that are unknown, hidden, not sponsors, or belong to unpublished events are ignored silently.

The Discover ribbon is app-wide and shows each sponsor once, whichever event's card comes first. The staff report (`GET /v1/admin/events/{id}/sponsor-report`, shown as "Sponsor engagement" in the admin Content tab) therefore gives venue page and message counts for that event and ribbon counts summed across every card with the sponsor's name. Counters can be inflated by anyone who calls the public endpoint directly. That is acceptable for a demo-grade signal, and a production version would need rate limiting.
