# Match alerts and score disputes

Team rankings open by default. Partner logos replace descriptive partner cards, with explicit demo artwork for fictional placements. The staff area shares the public footer, and discovery reads the same account display name as the profile dashboard.

Match cards omit source captions and staff estimate attribution. Delay links open the matching detailed alert; normal court updates already emit change-feed notifications, and showcase seeds backfill missing sample delay notifications. Only live cards show an orange unverified-result badge.

Signed-in users may submit one private score dispute per match with an optional bounded comment. Reports do not change scores. Event-scoped staff review and resolve disputes in Courts & clock. A new migration stores reports and enforces uniqueness; guest users receive a sign-in link. Railway predeploy applies the migration on merge.
